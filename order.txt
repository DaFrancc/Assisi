IN FLIGHT
---------

  ENG-213  Shadow casters — the gather honors maxDistance          2 pts


1. FINISH THE SUN'S CASTER PATH
-------------------------------

  ENG-214  Shadow casters — per-cascade lists replace the per-view sweep

    Same function and same file as ENG-213, and step 2 of the same research
    recommendation ENG-213 was step 1 of. Classify each caster once against all
    cascades into a 6-bit mask during the gather; each view then walks its own
    list instead of sweeping the whole span. ~7100 frustum tests become ~1183
    classifications plus list walks. Also produces the per-view caster entries
    ENG-215 needs later.


2. THE ATLAS'S REAL PREREQUISITES
---------------------------------

  ENG-86   Sky and atmosphere rendering
  ENG-142  Hemisphere ambient and the indirect-lighting provider seam   1 pt

    There is no skybox at all today, so outdoor scenes render against a clear
    colour, and mesh.frag's indirect term is a flat 0.03 constant — shadows over
    it read as black pits and would look worse than no shadows. No atlas visual
    gate means anything until this lands.

    ENG-142 also installs the indirect-lighting provider seam, which is the
    whole insurance policy for global illumination, and it is free here because
    that shader line is being rewritten anyway.


3. THE ATLAS — POINT AND SPOT SHADOWS
-------------------------------------

  ENG-139  Local-light shadow atlas + importance cap                8 pts
  ENG-140  Local-light shadow atlas — static-depth caching          4 pts
  ENG-141  Editor shadow diagnostics                                2 pts
  ENG-201  Sun shadow cadence — cascade validity and re-render      4 pts

    ENG-139's four formal blockers (ENG-200, ENG-80, ENG-136, ENG-84) are all
    Done. Spot = 1 face, point = 6 faces, one shared tiered atlas, top-N
    importance selection with per-type caps.

    The milestone closes at ENG-140, not at ENG-139: the cap alone bounds the
    cost, caching is what makes generous shadowed-light counts cheap. ENG-141 is
    blocked by 139; ENG-201 is blocked by 140.


4. THE LOD CHAIN, WHICH UNLOCKS SHADOW LOD
------------------------------------------

  ENG-209  Runtime LOD — screen-size selection with hysteresis

  ENG-210  GPU-driven F2b — LOD selection in mesh_cull.comp
