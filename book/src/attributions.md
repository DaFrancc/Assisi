# Attributions

Some files in `assets/` were not made for this engine. This page lists each
one, where it came from, and the credit its licence asks for.

**When you bring in an asset from outside, add it here** in the same change: the
file, what it is for, its title and author, where you got it, and its licence.
If the licence asks you to say what you changed, such as converting the format
or trimming it, say that too.

A game made with the engine ships every asset here outside `assets/editor/`,
which is editor content and never cooked into a game. For each one it ships,
the game has to give the same credit, for example in its own credits screen.

Your game does not have to include these assets if you do not wish to and you
may remove both the assets and the attributions.

## Images

### `assets/textures/moon.jpg`

The moon's albedo, drawn on the moon's disk in the sky.

- **Source:** NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio (Ernie Wright), *Moon Phase
  and Libration*, 2026: <https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5587/>
- **Data:** LROC WAC colour mosaic, NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University.
- **Licence:** not subject to copyright in the US. Credit is given as NASA
  asks. Nothing here implies NASA endorsement, and the NASA insignia and worm
  are not used.
